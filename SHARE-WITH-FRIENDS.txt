FORTNITE LOOT TRACKER 3.1.1 - WINDOWS x64

1. Extract the whole ZIP to a folder on your PC.
2. Open Fortnite Loot Tracker.exe.
3. Enable Fortnite replay recording, play a match, and keep this app open.

The app finds YOUR Windows account's Fortnite replay folder automatically.
For a different location, use the settings gear > Choose folder.
The first import may take a few minutes. Counts update after a replay finishes.
No separate .NET installation, sign-in, or administrator access is needed.
Supported package: Windows 10/11 on Intel/AMD x64. macOS builds are not included.

OVERVIEW: Your container total, chest artwork, latest match and live monitoring.
HISTORY: Select a match for container types; Export CSV saves your breakdowns.
TOKEN ODDS: Switch between Rest of season and My odds so far. Enter playtime,
Treasure Hours and party scenarios. Replay counts fill your own chest total.
The calculator shows both '1 in X' and percentage results. Rates are editable.
Replay counts update as new matches are imported. Use my replay counts restores
your own searched + broken chest total and Solo, without multiplying it by a party estimate.
Typing your own chest count keeps a manual scenario until you use replay counts
again. Party buttons are optional estimates, not measured teammate chest totals.
SETTINGS: Replay folder, scan now, subtle animations and always on top.

PLAYTIME & SQUAD: Recorded replay time in hours and minutes, with your own
regular + broken + Sprite chest total. This is recording duration, including
any recorded spectating; it is not time alive or all your Fortnite playtime.
Choose Count from > Apply to filter totals, history, CSV exports, replay-based
odds and this tab from that local calendar day onward. All time clears it.
The date and saved names persist when you close the app.
Type part of a teammate's name to see matching names found in your replays.
Choose a suggestion, then Save teammate. Arrow keys and Enter also select names.
You can also type an exact Epic display name. Partial names with detected matches
prompt you to select the recorded name, so prefixes do not hide their counts.
The table shows
shared recorded matches and observed regular/Sprite chests searched or broken
by that player on your team. Double-click a saved name, or select it and press
Enter, to see their searched/broken counts per shared match. This history uses
the same start-date filter. Right-click a saved row to remove the name.
Teammate tracking is experimental. Counts depend on what your replay recorded.
Distant actions, anonymous
names and older deleted source replays may be unavailable. 'Not recorded' means
no attributable chest actions were available; it does not prove zero openings.
This does not monitor friends while they play without you. Names stay local.
Version 3.1 reads additional chest updates from numbered map containers and
updates whose field descriptions arrive late. Available replays rescan once.
Cached history for recordings no longer on disk keeps its previous counts.

Totals include containers searched and broken containers attributed to you by
the replay. History and CSV export separate Searched from Broken. Recorded
vehicle hits can count under Broken, along with other destruction methods.
This does not identify the exact cause of destruction. Unknown destroyers and
destructions omitted by the replay cannot be counted. A searched container is
not counted a second time just because it is later destroyed.
Individual loot pieces are not counted.
Replay formats can change after Fortnite updates. Unavailable matches are marked
and excluded from totals. Odds do not detect whether a token actually dropped.
The calculator assumes independent rolls. Treasure Hours are entered manually.
Party scenarios assume equal chest counts and shared rewards. This is a planning
assumption. Past misses do not increase the chance on the next chest.
Odds autofill includes searched + attributed broken chests, including Sprite
chests. Ammo boxes and other containers are excluded. The model assumes the same
drop rate applies to broken chests; that eligibility is not verified.
The reference rate is 1 in 137,500, not a live feed of Epic event settings.
Default forecast days count to December 5, 2026; edit the days for your own plan.
Rate reference: https://themathbehindeverything.com/fortnite-pirate-token/

PRIVACY / SHARING
This ZIP contains only the application, this guide and open-source licenses.
It does NOT contain the creator's replay files, match history, settings or login.
Your history, start date, saved teammate names and settings are stored on your PC at:
%LOCALAPPDATA%/FortniteLootTracker
The app reads replay files without changing them or uploading them.
To share it, upload the ZIP to a file-sharing service you use and send its link.
Re-extract a newer package when you want to update; saved history stays on the PC.
